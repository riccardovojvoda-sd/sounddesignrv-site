The founder who sold coffee for 50 lire in 1976, the children who speak of generational complicity, operators at 6:30 in the morning, thirty vans and 800,000 kilometres a year, the transformation into a Benefit Corporation. Seven minutes that alternate drone shots, car-mounted camera, footage on site and interviews with the protagonists: heterogeneous material, held together by sound. Production sound in the company and in the van, clean dialogue over the noise of the vehicles, emotional library music tuned to the story.
