A corporate documentary, a family story, with a polished, cinematic sound. The sound design works on the transitions. Production sound on site and in the driver's cab gives us high-quality narrative voice-overs and clean dialogue over the sound of the vehicles and the emotional music cut to the story.

The material: drone, car-mounted camera, footage on site and the voices of the founder and his children. Fifty years of CDA, from the 50-lire coffee of 1976 to Benefit Corporation status, thirty vans and 800,000 kilometres a year.
