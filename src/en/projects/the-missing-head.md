Early 2000s: David Hanson and Andrew Olney build the first AI human replica, an android head with the features of the writer Philip K. Dick. "Android Phil" tours the United States, stuns Comic-Con 2005 alongside Richard Linklater, then mysteriously disappears, and with it the partnership of its creators falls apart.

A CROMO documentary, with footage shot in Hong Kong with Hanson, that uses this story to question the ethics of innovation. So far I have done the sound of the trailer: dialogue, sound design and mix.
