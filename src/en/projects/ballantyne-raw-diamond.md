Ballantyne's Raw Diamond capsule tells a return to the origins of Scottish cashmere: shearing, raw wool, knitting machines, the hands that finish the garment. The sound builds the material (fibres, needles, fabric) and makes room for the narrating voice, up to the Made in Scotland mark.
