A graduate in Music and New Technologies from the **Trieste Conservatory**, I started working in production sound and post-production in 2006, after years as a music producer and mixer.

In 2010 I continued my training at SAE Institute in Milan and then in Paris, where I spent four years in **post-production for short films and documentaries**, absorbing the attention to sonic detail typical of the French film school.

Back in Italy in 2015, I carried on in production sound and audio post-production for the corporate and advertising sector, working with **[CROMO]({{ sito.social.cromo }})** and brands such as **Maserati, Diadora, Stellantis, Everli, The North Face** and many more.

Over the years I have handled the sound of many independent documentaries and short films and, more recently, of a **[feature-length thriller in 5.1](/en/projects/sottopelle/)**, covering production sound, dialogue editing and sound design. I also work on **[immersive content in 5.1 and 7.1](/en/projects/arcadia-earth/) for planetariums and surround installations**, combining technical skill, musical sensitivity and a narrative approach to sound, always in service of the picture.

**Since 2021 I have taught Audio Post-Production at H-Farm College and IUAV Venice**, passing on a working method that brings together technique, listening and creativity.

I keep my workflow up to date, adopting AI tools to streamline sound restoration, editing and mixing while holding to the quality and delivery standards of cinema, TV, streaming and the record industry.

I work remotely or on site with studios, production companies and artists in Italy and abroad. **I speak English, French and Italian** and understand Spanish.

## Sonic horizon

I design and build sound as an integral part of the visual story, taking care of every layer, from dialogue to the design of ambiences and non-diegetic effects, to guarantee coherence, intelligibility and emotional balance.

I work collaboratively with directors, editors and content creators, but I am also fully independent: I can carry the whole creative and technical side through to a rich, engaging result that fits the style of the product.
